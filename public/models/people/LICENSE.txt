The people in this folder are Microsoft Rocketbox avatars
(https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox): Male_Adult_08 (man-1), Male_Adult_01 (man-2),
Female_Adult_08 (woman-1) and Female_Adult_01 (woman-2). Their textures were converted from TGA to JPEG
(the opacity map split into colour and coverage); the models are the original "_facial" FBX files.
